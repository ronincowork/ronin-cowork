# Get started

Three steps take you from a fresh Ronin to Agents working for you: connect a harness, say what
you are working on, and start a Team. Your phone's browser works as well as your computer's.
On a hosted office, press **Setup** on Ronin Home: the steps are **Harness**, **Workspace** and
**Team**, in any order, with the Helpful Assistant beside each one ([Ronin Setup](setup-workbench.md)).
Self-hosted, the same three are Home's **Harnesses**, **Workspaces** and **Teams** doors. A step
gets a check once it is true in your office, however you did it.

## 1. Connect a harness

Pick the harness you already use: Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, and others. Sign in with
your own subscription or API account. One is enough to start. [Provider sign-in](harness-sign-in.md)
walks through it. The first one you sign in becomes the default every new Agent launches on;
signing in another later doesn't change it. Change it on **Harnesses > Defaults**.

## 2. Say what you are working on

Make a Workspace for your project: first what it is, then where it lives, its folders or
repositories. Your Teams work in it. See [Workspaces](../using-ronin/workspaces.md).

## 3. Start a Team

Start a Team in your Workspace: its name, what it is for, and its lead's harness. The lead
builds out the rest. See [New Team](../using-ronin/new-team.md). **Launch your own** in Settings
starts a single Agent.

## Say hello

Type a short message into the tile, such as:

> Reply with exactly: Ronin Agent ready

When the Agent answers, you're running. From here, ask for real work, start a Team from
the **Teams** door, or come back to [Ronin Setup](setup-workbench.md) for the optional steps.

If the Agent asks you to sign in, go back to **Harnesses**, finish signing in, and
launch again.
