# Start a Team

A Team groups Agents around shared work, always in one [Workspace](workspaces.md). Open **New Team**
from the launch flow, or the Team step in Setup: it is the same form.

## Set up the work

1. **Name and objective.** The Team name, its title, the **Team objective** (what the lead picks
   up and starts on) and the **Team instructions** (standing directions every Agent in the Team reads).
2. **Workspace.** The Workspace the Team is dedicated to. Its settings are what the Team's Agents
   inherit; change one Team's afterwards in its Configuration on the Team page.
3. **Agents.** The Team starts with its lead already in the list, named after the first word of the
   Team name (`front-2` gives `front_lead`); the name follows the Team name as you type until you
   edit it, and the lead can be removed like any Agent. Add the other Agents you want, each with its
   harness. Left blank, an Agent's harness, model and mandate are the Team's, which it copies from
   its Workspace when it is made.
4. **Epic.** Pick one of the Workspace's open epics to hand to the Team, or leave **A new epic** and
   give it a title (the Team's title if you leave it blank). The lead is told its epic and the objective.

Then press **Launch**.

Names must use lowercase letters, digits, underscores, and hyphens. If an Agent name is
already in use, the form asks for a different name before it creates the Team.

The Team opens in its own browser tab as soon as its record is created. Workspaces requested
for new Agents fill as their sessions join the live Team roster. With no new Agents, the tab
opens Commons and New Agent. With one, it opens that Agent and Commons. With two or more,
the first two Agents take the two workspaces. If only some Agents start successfully, the form
names those that started and those that failed. The Team remains available; add the failed
Agents there instead of launching the whole Team again.

## Membership and shared work

Live session memberships determine who belongs to a Team. A Team's saved record holds its
identity, objective, instructions, Workspace and held work; it does not keep a second list of members or
leads. An Agent can belong to more than one Team. Changing membership does not restart the
Agent or replace the instructions it received at birth.

Use the Team's [workbench](workbench.md) to open its Agents and Commons. Coordinate through
[work records](work-record.md) and [wipeboards](wipeboards.md).
