# RONIN_UTILITY — where everything is, and how the owner drives it

The quick answers for "where is…", "how do I…" and "what is this" about Ronin itself. Answer from here; the fuller pages are one `ls` away on the shelves.

## The pages

- **The home page** (`/`) has three doors: **Campaign**, **Coworks**, **Agents**. Each door launches what is loaded; its chip opens a selector where a star sets the default and the footer makes a new one — **New Campaign**, **New Cowork**, **New Agent**. This is where a fuller new Agent or Team is made.
- **The bar** on every page has two doors, **Ronin** (the Campaign's all-Coworks page) and **Coworks** (your Cowork's workbench), the **か New** button for a quick new session, **⚙** for the cowork commons, and the **2 ⇄ 4** button for the workbench shape.
- **On a phone** the coworkspace is three steps: pick the Cowork, pick the Agent, drive it in a full-screen tile with the composer docked at the bottom.

## The workbench

One page format: a **selector column** (the roster: the team commons card first, then each Agent, then ＋ Add team member) beside **two or four workspaces**. A **workspace** is a numbered slot; a **surface** is what it holds. Select a workspace, then click a roster card, or drag the card onto any workspace, and that surface opens there; the previous one is back in the column. An empty workspace says *Workspace*. Ronin remembers the arrangement per page.

| Surface | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **terminal tile** | one Agent: its live terminal, composer, Output selector, Control dial, work record |
| **team commons** | this team: **Docs** (what agents listed, plans, docs by project root) · **Wipeboard** · **Agent Message Queue** · **Team Configuration** (the team's Routines, kit, launch defaults) |
| **cowork commons** (⚙) | this install and owner: usage stats · **Account** (Configuration · Appearance · Release & update · Hotwords · Koshi · gbrain · Log out) · Desk profile · Project roots · Archived · Help desk · Keypad |
| **campaign commons** | the Campaign: Campaign · Project roots · Team roster · Templates · **Routines** (the switchboard that fills new teams) |
| **new session** | the launcher, placed by か New or ＋ Add team member; the newborn lands in that workspace |

**Where to send the owner:** teams → the **Coworks** door · settings, account, look, updates → **⚙ → Account** · which Routines a team runs → **Team Configuration**; for new teams, the Campaign's **Routines** · project roots and templates → the campaign commons · a fuller new Agent or Team → the home page doors · a quick session → **か New**.

## The tile

- **Output.** *Locked* is the live terminal. Other outputs (Terminal Mirror, Detailed, Condensed, Conversation, Agent Summary) are record-fed views that arrive with Ronin Services.
- **Scroll** with the wheel; the ↓ pill returns to the bottom. Typing while scrolled up does nothing until you are back at the bottom or press Escape.
- **Copy from a Locked tile:** hold **Shift** (**Option** on a Mac) while dragging to select, then copy as usual (⌘C / Ctrl+C). Without the key, the running app takes the drag and nothing is selected.
- **The composer** under the tile sends a message to the Agent; it is separate from the terminal's own input.
- **Control** 👤 · 👁 · 🤖 says who else may read or drive this session; only the owner turns it.
- **The work record** button opens the Agent's account of its task; the Docs tab lists the documents it is working on, this README among them.

## Feedback

The **Feedback** button, top right, sends the owner's words to the Ronin team: what they like, what they want added, how they use Ronin, and a comment. When the owner voices a wish, a complaint or a "how do I", answer, then point them there. Their experience is what shapes the next release.
