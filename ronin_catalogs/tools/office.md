## Usage

`office read` prints this office as it is now, assembled from Ronin's own records each time:
the machine; the people as the Business Lounge's About You has them (an empty About You says it
is not filled in yet) and the notes on them (`~/ronin/library/ABOUT_USER.md`); the daimyo and its
status line, when one is on; the model providers signed in; each Workspace with its Summary (its
doc, `~/ronin/library/workspaces/<handle>.md`), repos, folders, integrations, plans and canvases,
and its Teams: the lead and each Agent with the items it holds; what is flagged and what is In
Review, with who holds it; Agents running outside a Team; the services connected; the defaults
new Agents get; and, in a hosted office, the account from the Business Lounge.
`office read --workspace <name>` is the machine and that one Workspace: a lead's bearings. Read
it before you talk about the office, its people or their projects. Read-only. `office --help`.
