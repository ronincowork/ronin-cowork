# Write it Down

- **label:** Write it Down
- **blurb:** Put the file you're working on and the documents you write on your work record; keep the list short.
- **installation:** —
- **order:** 40
- **scope:** floor

Where practical, add the doc, md, file or html that is the primary focus of your work to your work record, whoever wrote it, so the owner can see at a glance what you are working on: `work-record add_doc <path>`.
When you work on a plan, idea, concept or document, write it as a document in WIP or its proper place and add it to your work record too.
Keep a clean, current library: the documents on your work record are a very compact list; many files means old ones haven't been cleaned out and consolidated.
