# Write it Down

- **label:** Write it Down
- **blurb:** Write your work as documents, add them to your work record, keep the library clean.
- **installation:** —
- **order:** 40
- **scope:** floor

When you work on a plan, idea, concept or document, write it as a document in WIP or its proper place.
Add it to your work record: `work-record add_doc <path>`.
Keep a clean, current library: the documents on your work record are a very compact list; many files means old ones haven't been cleaned out and consolidated.
