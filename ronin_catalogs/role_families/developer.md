# developer

The coding shelf. A session may RiffOnIt, DraftPlan, CutCode, ChaseBug or CheckWork
without becoming a different session — the session_role is the mutable axis, and this
family is just where the coding kind of work is grouped.

**A family is PRESENTATION, not structure** (R35, 2026-08-23). Nothing here rides a
launch or a letter: each session_role carries its own launch constants, and a session's
identity — where it has one — is contextual to its Team. This
shelf groups the buttons, and doubles as a Build-Team template: building a developer
team starts from this family, its roles as the menu.

**`QuarterBack` is the pinned `default_lead_role`** — presented first, and the
suggested first launch when a team is built from this shelf. Its launch carries the
team-building SOP (`ronin_sops/teams.md`). The pin is a default and NEVER the
`team_lead` designation on a live session: the owner can make any session — the
secretary included — a team's lead.
