# assistant

The seats that answer questions rather than cut work. One assists the OWNER
(`PersonalAssistant`, powered by gbrain); one assists the HOUSE (`MikaAssist`, Ronin's
own business). They do not trade jobs, and this shelf is the thing they have in common.

**A family is PRESENTATION, not structure** (R35, 2026-08-23): it groups the buttons on
the New Session board and seeds a Build-Team template, and contributes nothing to a
launch — each session_role carries its own constants. No `default_lead_role` is pinned;
the shelf is small enough to need no suggestion.
