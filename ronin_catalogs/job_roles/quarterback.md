# quarterback

Coordinates other sessions — dispatch, unblock, report upward. **The role that proved the
axis**: it was always a role wearing an act's name, and the owner's own word for it —
*"I want to call this my quarter back."* It was `WatchCrew` before that, which obeyed the
verb+object grammar and was still wrong: "watch" reads as *observe* on a board where
`CheckWork` is the one that observes, and this job is the opposite of watching. A
quarterback reads the field, calls the play and does not run it.

**Still bound by every dial**: a 👤 session is invisible to it.

It shelves no task of its own and launches blank — coordinating is what it *is*, not
something it is doing this hour.
