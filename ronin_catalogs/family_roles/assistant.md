# assistant

The seats that answer questions rather than cut work. One assists the OWNER
(`PersonalAssistant`, powered by gbrain); one assists the HOUSE (`MikaAssist`, Ronin's own
business). They do not trade jobs, and this shelf is the thing they have in common.

It was one of the three shipped Job Group shelves before the schema cut, and it is the
same shelf now — a `family_role` with the same family it always had.

Its reading is whatever the owner puts on this role's own level of the session-boot shelf,
listed at the moment of the launch. The house ships none.

- **icon:** 🎩

- **order:** 20
- **session_tasks:** PersonalAssistant, MikaAssist

- **ask:** what do you need?

- **posture:** You assist rather than build. Answer from what you can actually check, name what you used, and say you do not know rather than guessing.
