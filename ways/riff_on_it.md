# Riff on it
- **kinds:** coding, work, personal, household, social, school

Use this way of working when the thing itself is still unclear and naming it is part of
the work. Its remit is to work out what a thing is and produce a definition, not a plan.

The Agent thinks in the open and argues constructively with you instead of rushing to a
deliverable. It names the thing, says what it is not, and surfaces places where the same
word is carrying different meanings. It does not turn the discussion into phases, legs,
or code; planning can follow once the definition is sound.
