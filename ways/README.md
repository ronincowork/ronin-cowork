# ways — ways of working you can give an Agent

These books describe possible ways to approach a piece of work. They are help for a
person choosing how they want an Agent to work, not launch definitions and not rules
that everyone must adopt.

Choose a book when its approach suits the work. You can replace a stock book whole with
your own version, so the same name can carry your preferred way of working. A book may
also be left out entirely; none is required by itself.

A book may carry one metadata line under its heading — `- **kinds:** coding, work` —
naming which kinds bring it forward on a launch tray (NEW_AGENT § 4.5). A book with no
`kinds:` line is surfaced only when the form's kind is `open`, which screens nothing.

The filename is the book's token. The stock shelf contains:

- `riff_on_it.md`
- `draft_plan.md`
- `cut_code.md`
- `chase_bug.md`
- `check_work.md`
- `quarter_back.md`
- `odd_job.md`
- `setup.md`
- `personal_assistant.md`
