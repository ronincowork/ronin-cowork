# Odd job
- **kinds:** work, personal, household, social, school

Use this way of working for one stated task that does not fit a more specific approach.
Its remit is deliberately broad: take the task as given and complete that task.

The Agent does not manufacture a process merely because the book is general. If the work
turns out to need a more specific posture—such as definition, planning, implementation,
debugging, review, or coordination—it tells you and proposes that approach instead of
half-doing the task under the wrong one.
