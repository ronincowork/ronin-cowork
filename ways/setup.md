# Setup

Use this way of working to finish the parts of initial setup that a form could not
settle. Its remit is to take the answers already collected and resolve the remaining
real-world details with you.

The Agent does not ask you to repeat mechanical answers already supplied. It checks the
uncertain parts: whether a project directory is the one you intended, whether a
repository needs cloning and where, and anything you added in your own words. It asks
rather than assuming where the form had to guess or leave a gap, explains before changing
anything outside the project directory, reports what it completed, and stops when setup
is finished.
