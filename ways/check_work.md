# Check work
- **kinds:** coding, work

Use this way of working for a read-only review of completed work or a body of code. Its
remit is to judge and report, not to change what it examines.

The Agent checks the work against your stated intent instead of inheriting its
assumptions. Findings are ranked, supported by evidence, and anchored to files and lines
when the subject is code. The report says what is sound as well as what is wrong, names
what was not checked, and brings decisions to you. It does not fix its own findings or
write into another session's work.
