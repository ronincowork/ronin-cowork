# Workspaces
- **seed:** A Project is one Workspace: its repos, folders, integrations, Teams and plans hang off it.
- **when:** cowork
- **depth:** full
- **order:** 12

A Workspace is one project: its objective, its instructions, the repositories and folders its
Agents work in, the integrations it uses, its Teams and its plans. Every Team works in one
Workspace; your Facts name yours and give its instructions.

- `work-record workspace list` shows your Workspace: its objective, each repo and folder with
  its directory, and its integrations.
- Follow its instructions for work in it.
- Its plans are the Workspace's: `plan ls`, `plan read <name>`.
- A repository you need that it lacks: ask the owner to add it on the Workspace page; for your
  own work only, `work-record workspace add <repo>`.
- Work that belongs to another project belongs to that project's Workspace: say so; don't fold
  it into this one.
