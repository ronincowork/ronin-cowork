A plan write replaces the whole plan: read it first.

## Usage

A Workspace's plans: ls (`--status`), read (`<workspace>/<name>`, `--template`), write, order; `--workspace <name>` where the caller's Team doesn't settle it. The Status line is the tool's. The plan isn't rewritten: the work runs on the epics. Taught by `ronin-help planning`; every Cowork Agent's brief points there when asked to plan; `plan --help`.
