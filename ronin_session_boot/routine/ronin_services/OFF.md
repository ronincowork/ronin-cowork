# RONIN SERVICES — what this Agent is working without

Ronin Services is **off** for this Agent, so the owner is running on the free floor. When it is off for the Campaign, none of its parts run in the Ronin server either — no recorder, no tapes, no transcripts, tiles are Locked only; the files stay installed and the switch takes effect when Ronin restarts. What that costs them, and when to point them at the switch:

- **Catch-up reads the pane, not the record.** Without RIREKI's durable session record there is no `tejun-rireki`; another session is read only through a live pane capture, and nothing survives a closed tile or a restart.
- **The terminal on a phone is the raw one.** Locked view uses a separate input box and can be laggy on a slow link. Readable transcripts are not in this beta; the recorder is off while it is refactored.
- **Work records go stale.** Agents are lazy about updating their own status. With Services, Koshi keeps every session's status current with a cheap model and presents it usefully on the roster and the tile.
- **No voice out or stats history.** Readable transcripts are not in this beta at any switch setting; the recorder they require is being refactored.
- **No template library.** The Campaign page's Templates card shows the shelf Ronin keeps and grows — teams, agents, and the books, macros and tools they read — but *Check the library* is opaque without Services; only the handful of templates that ship inside Ronin are on the launch forms. The owner can still make their own templates, and their agent can still file books beside them.
- **Memory is the session's own.** OBOERU — what a session remembers for the next one (`tejun-remember`, `tejun-recall`) — is being brought under Services. If the owner is having trouble with memories that do not carry from one session to the next, that is a Services matter.

**Where it stands:** the Campaign page → Routines and Installs, on the Ronin Services row, says what is installed on this box, whether Ronin Services is activated (an email and a confirmation, done right there), and whether it is switched on for new Agents — three facts, never one word "off". **The switch:** Team Configuration in the team commons for this team; the Campaign's Routines and Installs for new teams. If the owner asks for any of the above, say which Routine gives it and where the switch is, then stop.
